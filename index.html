My roadmap to 2030

These are my goals for each year of the degree, not things already done. I will update them as I complete each one.

  1. Year 1 · 2026–27

    Build the foundation

    • Aim for a strong CGPA in the core subjects
    • Learn the basics of pharmacovigilance and case processing by mid 2027
    • Get fluent in MS Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Canva
    • Start GPAT and GATE preparation early
  2. Year 2 · 2027–28

    Learn patient care in a hospital

    • Hospital Pharmacy internship at AIIMS, New Delhi
    • Practise medication reconciliation, therapeutic drug monitoring and patient counselling
    • Understand clinical trials, GCP and regulatory guidelines (CDSCO, ICH) by 2028
  3. Year 3 · 2028–29

    Get research exposure

    • Research internship at CSIR-IGIB, Delhi
    • Practise adverse drug reaction reporting
    • Learn quality control and pharmaceutical analysis
    • Add data analysis and statistics, plus ChemDraw and literature search tools
  4. Year 4 · 2029–30

    Step into industry

    • Pharmacovigilance or Quality Control internship at Sun Pharma or Biocon
    • Learn aseptic compounding (IV, IM, TPN, chemotherapy preparation)
    • Sit GPAT / GATE and apply for placements, with Novartis as the top target
  5. After 2030

    Specialise and lead

    • M.Pharm in Pharmaceutics or Pharmacology, or MS in Pharmaceutical Sciences
    • Drug Safety / Pharmacovigilance Associate role
    • Grow toward regulatory affairs and leadership
